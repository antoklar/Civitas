About Civitas

Your government, explained.

Civitas is a free, nonpartisan tool that helps you find and understand every elected official who represents you — from city hall to Capitol Hill — using only public government data.

Mission

Most people can name their president. Far fewer can name their state legislator, their county commissioner, or the member of Congress who actually votes on the bills that affect their daily lives. That gap isn't a knowledge problem — it's an access problem. Government data is public, but it's scattered across dozens of disconnected websites, PDFs, and databases.

Civitas exists to close that gap. Enter an address, and we pull together who represents you, how they vote, who funds their campaigns, and how to reach them — all in one place, in language that doesn't require a political science degree to understand.

How It Works
1

Enter your address

We geocode it to find your exact federal, state, and local districts.

2

We match you to your officials

Every representative tied to those districts is pulled from public records — no paywalls, no guesswork.

3

You see the full picture

Voting record, committee assignments, sponsored legislation, top campaign donors, and direct contact info, all on one profile.
